# Pseudonymisation

A derived concept — not in the booklet glossary but recurring as a load-bearing technique across the Phase I + II corpus. The Swiss FADP/DSG term for replacing identifying attributes in a dataset with synthetic identifiers, such that re-identification requires additional information held separately.

## Where the corpus uses it

- [[smart-parking]] — part of the canonical privacy-by-design lexicon (alongside low resolution, masking, edge computing, instant deletion, data minimisation).
- [[ai-in-education]] — extends the same lexicon to data-of-minors contexts.
- [[digital-eye-clinic]] — necessary for sharing diagnostic imagery across the hospital-research boundary.
- [[building-permits]] — pseudonymisation of building-application data was a precondition for the model training corpus.

## Regulatory framing

A [[data-protection-officer]] (DPO) typically requires that **the pseudonymisation process itself be auditable** — a recurring requirement in Phase II health-sector projects. See [[dsg-fadp]] for the underlying legal anchor.
