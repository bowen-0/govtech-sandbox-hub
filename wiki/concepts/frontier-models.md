# Frontier Models

> *"Highly advanced, large-scale AI models (e.g. large language or multimodal models) representing the current state of the art. They are trained on extensive datasets using substantial computational resources and underpin many generative AI applications, while also introducing heightened requirements for safety, governance, and regulation."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary p. 26.

The Build & Share booklet (§04 Technology, "Combine frontier models and open-source solutions strategically") makes the *combination* of frontier models + open-source models a strategic recommendation rather than a binary choice — requirements vary across data-protection, performance, cost, and scalability.

The single most rigorous test of frontier-model behaviour in the corpus is [[building-permits]]: 8 frontier models benchmarked across 3,336 evaluations using [[llm-as-a-judge]]. Headline finding: **no single model wins** — model-agnostic architecture is mandatory.
