# Deepfakes

> *"Audio, image, or video content generated or manipulated using AI to create realistic but artificially produced representations of individuals or events."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary p. 26.

Appears in the Phase II corpus as a **submitted-but-discontinued** project — deepfake detection in the context of identity fraud was one of three Phase II projects "not pursued further by mutual agreement" ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03 Additional Projects, p. 15). The discontinuation reasons differ from the analysis-only outcomes of [[medical-documentation]] and [[inspection-robots]]: deepfake detection was not implementable in the sandbox format primarily for partner-availability reasons.

> [!gap] No executed pilot
> No sandbox report exists for this concept. The page captures the booklet's framing but the corpus has no concrete operational evidence.
