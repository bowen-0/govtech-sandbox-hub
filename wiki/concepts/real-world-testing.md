# Real-World Testing

> *"The evaluation of an AI system under real operating conditions using actual data, processes, and organisational contexts, in order to validate technical, regulatory, and operational aspects in a practical setting."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary p. 26.

The defining methodological commitment of the sandbox. The booklet's opening contrast ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §01):

> *"In contrast to many international approaches, the focus lies on **real-world testing** rather than purely regulatory assessments."*

**Three of five Phase II projects achieved real-world testing**: [[bridge-monitoring]], [[digital-eye-clinic]], [[building-permits]]. The other two ([[medical-documentation]], [[inspection-robots]]) pivoted to pure regulatory analysis — a legitimate sandbox outcome but a distinct project class. The wiki distinguishes them via the `status: completed | analysis-only` frontmatter on project pages.

> [!tension] Real-world testing vs. analysis-only
> The sandbox treats both as successful outputs, but they answer different questions. Real-world testing produces operational evidence (deployment patterns, cost data, integration findings). Analysis-only projects produce regulatory clarity (compliance pathways, supervisory-authority engagement). When citing a Phase II lesson, check which kind of evidence supports it.
