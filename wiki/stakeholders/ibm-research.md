# IBM Research

Research arm of IBM. Partnered with the Canton of Zurich sandbox on the Phase I [[infrastructure-maintenance]] project — drone inspections of military infrastructure with computer vision.

## Why IBM Research is notable in the corpus

The sandbox engagement produced a **structured, annotated dataset for infrastructure inspection** that IBM Research subsequently published as a public benchmark on **Hugging Face**. This is the corpus's canonical case of a sandbox project producing reusable data infrastructure *beyond its own engagement*.

Per [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §01, p. 5:

> *"In the drone inspection project, IBM Research developed a structured, annotated dataset for infrastructure inspection, which serves as a benchmark for future AI models and is publicly accessible via scientific publications and platforms such as Hugging Face."*

This canonical instance of *data reuse beyond the sandbox engagement* is the empirical foundation of the lesson [[data-access-is-the-most-powerful-lever]].
