# Swissmedic

The Swiss Agency for Therapeutic Products — the federal supervisory authority for medicines and medical devices. **Critically relevant** to Phase II's healthcare focus: any AI system in a clinical workflow may fall under the *medical device* classification, bringing it within Swissmedic's regulatory remit.

## Role in the sandbox

Per [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03 (medical documentation, p. 10):

> *"Collaboration with additional stakeholders, including Swissmedic, the Department of Health of the Canton of Zurich, and the eHealth working group of swissICT, was a key success factor in deepening the use case from multiple perspectives."*

The sandbox engagement with Swissmedic is recognised in the booklet as a model for the broader pattern: AI providers gain regulatory clarity by accessing supervisory authorities they would not otherwise reach. See [[regulatory-roundtable]] for the convening format.
