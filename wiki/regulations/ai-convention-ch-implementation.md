# Swiss Implementation of the Council of Europe AI Convention

Switzerland's implementation of the Council of Europe's *Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law* — the first international legally-binding instrument specifically on AI, opened for signature 2024. The Swiss Federal Council's implementation decision came in early 2025; this is referenced in the booklet as the *Bundesratsentscheid zur KI-Regulierung*.

## Relevance to the corpus

The booklet ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §04 Legal, "AI does not operate in a legal vacuum"):

> *"Existing legal frameworks, e.g. in data protection and sector-specific regulation, are central in all projects and already address many of the key issues. AI-specific regulation, such as the implementation of the AI Convention into Swiss law, complements this framework in a targeted manner, especially in areas where AI systems affect fundamental rights."*

This positions the Convention as the **fundamental-rights backstop** to sector-specific regulation rather than as a replacement for it. The framing matters for any system that processes data in ways that affect individual rights — which is the entire Phase II health-sector portfolio.

## Sandbox impact

The booklet ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §05 "Policy impulses") credits the sandbox with concrete input into the *Bundesratsentscheid zur KI-Regulierung* — sandbox findings on testing environments, cross-border collaboration, and sector-specific compliance fed into the federal-level decision.
