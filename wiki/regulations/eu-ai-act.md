# EU AI Act

> *"EU regulation governing AI systems based on a risk-based approach. It defines requirements for high-risk AI systems, transparency obligations, and prohibitions of certain applications."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary p. 26.

The defining EU instrument of the Phase II regulatory landscape. Where Phase I was dominated by Swiss law, Phase II projects engaged the AI Act as their primary regulatory frame — most notably [[inspection-robots]], whose entire project objective was to **systematically clarify EU AI Act + [[machinery-regulation]] interaction** for autonomous-robotics market access.

## Phase II projects engaging the AI Act

- [[inspection-robots]] — risk-based classification of autonomous inspection systems; interaction with the EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 was the central regulatory novelty.
- [[building-permits]] — peripheral engagement; primary Swiss-administrative-law framing.

## Cross-border collaboration

The booklet (§03, p. 14) records that the [[inspection-robots]] project engaged in **first-time cross-border collaboration** with Germany's *Bundesnetzagentur* and Liechtenstein's *Office for Digital Innovation* — setting a precedent for the cross-jurisdictional sandbox engagements the Phase III ecosystem strategy [[zh-ai-ecosystem-strategy-2026-2029]] envisions.
