# EMBAG — Federal Act on the Use of Electronic Means

The Swiss Federal Act on the Use of Electronic Means for Government Tasks (*Bundesgesetz über den Einsatz elektronischer Mittel zur Erfüllung von Behördenaufgaben*, EMBAG). SR 172.019, in force 17 March 2023. Regulates Swiss e-government infrastructure — most notably **the disclosure of administrative source code** (open-source by default) and the publication of administrative data.

## Why it matters for the corpus

[[building-permits]] explicitly engages EMBAG because the prototype is being developed with public funds for use in administrative procedures. Under EMBAG, the source code of public-administration software is generally required to be published as open-source — affecting how the prototype's deployment to *eBaugesucheZH* (the cantonal building-permit platform) is structured.

The implication generalises: any sandbox prototype that subsequently becomes part of public administration is presumptively subject to EMBAG's open-source disclosure requirement. This is one of the reasons [[digital-eye-clinic]] also went the open-source route — though that project's primary driver was the absence of fit-for-purpose commercial alternatives ([[intrapreneurship]]).
