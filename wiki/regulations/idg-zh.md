# IDG ZH — Cantonal Information and Data Protection Act (Zurich)

The Canton of Zurich's *Informations- und Datenschutzgesetz* (IDG). Applies whenever a **public body in Canton Zurich** is the data controller — the cantonal counterpart to the federal [[dsg-fadp]] (which applies when a private provider is the controller).

## Active revision noted in [[building-permits]]

The IDG ZH is **currently being revised** to add a transparency register for *Algorithmische Entscheidungsfindungssysteme (AES)* / algorithmic decision-making systems with fundamental-rights impact. [[building-permits]] flags this as load-bearing for future AI deployments in cantonal administrative procedures — the revised IDG ZH is likely to be the most relevant Swiss data-protection update for sandbox-aligned applications.
