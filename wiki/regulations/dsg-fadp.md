# Swiss FADP / DSG — Federal Act on Data Protection

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP in English, *Datenschutzgesetz* / DSG in German). Revised version in force since September 2023. The dominant Swiss data-protection statute, applicable whenever a **private provider** is the data controller — paired with [[idg-zh]] when a **public body** is the data controller.

## Key articles cited in the corpus

- **Art. 21 FADP** — Automated individual decisions; right to human review. Cited in [[building-permits]] as the reason fully automated rejection of a building application would likely breach the FADP. Also referenced in [[machine-translation]] and Phase II healthcare projects.

## Phase I usage

The booklet's "consistent Swiss legal stack" appears in every Phase I project:

1. **Cantonal IDG** ([[idg-zh]]) — when a public body controls the data.
2. **Federal FADP/DSG** (this regulation) — when a private provider controls the data.
3. **Commissioned-data-processing contracts** (*Auftragsdatenbearbeitung*) — for any third-party tool.
