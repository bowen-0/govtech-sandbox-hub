# Digital Eye Clinic

AI diagnostics for **diabetic retinopathy** screening in clinical practice. One of two Phase II healthcare projects (the other is [[medical-documentation]]), and the one that achieved real-world clinical testing. Test partners: *Augenklinik Stadtspital Zürich* + *Spross Stiftung für Augenheilkunde*.

## Outcome — three concrete deliverables

1. **A practice-tested evaluation framework** for AI diagnostic solutions, applicable beyond ophthalmology to other medical specialisations.
2. **A modular AI diagnostic platform built on open-source technologies** — because no commercial provider met the stringent requirements of a public hospital. The project explicitly demonstrated **intrapreneurship** and in-house development within the public healthcare sector.
3. **Regulatory, data-protection, and ethical best practices** for AI diagnostics in hospitals — transferable beyond the specific use case.

## Key learnings

- Diabetic retinopathy is rising in patient volume; AI-enabled screening relieves medical staff and improves efficiency.
- Open-source platforms can be a viable procurement alternative when commercial offerings cannot meet public-hospital requirements (data protection, transparency, modifiability).
- The evaluation framework is reusable across medical AI deployments — one of the most transferable artefacts in Phase II.
