# Smart Parking

Camera-based detection of free parking spaces using image recognition. The Phase I project that codified the operational playbook for computer vision in Swiss public-sector deployments — covering camera placement, image-handling under the Swiss legal stack, and the canonical **privacy-by-design vocabulary** (low resolution, masking, edge computing, instant deletion, data minimisation) that the rest of the Phase I corpus subsequently reuses.

## Outcome

Productive deployment in **Frauenfeld**, with subsequent roll-out to additional cantons. Cited in [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] as one of the canonical Phase I projects that scaled beyond the sandbox engagement itself.

## Key learnings

- The **partner-bottleneck** pattern: property-owner access to mount cameras is harder to secure than the AI itself.
- Privacy-by-design lexicon defined here recurs in [[ai-in-education]], [[digital-eye-clinic]], and is referenced in the Build & Share insights synthesis ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §04 Technology).
- Image recognition on the physical environment is a *much easier* visual modality than the plan-document understanding examined later in [[building-permits]].
