# Autonomous Systems

Regulatory guide for autonomous machinery — drones and autonomous agricultural systems — under the pre-EU-Machinery-Regulation Swiss legal framework. The canonical Phase I source for the lesson **"integrate regulation early into product development."** Retrofitting agricultural vehicles for automation required full re-certification of the product, demonstrating that late-stage regulatory fixes drive flawed business models.

## Outcome

Published regulatory guide. Cited prominently in [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §04 Legal as the foundational evidence for the *regulate-early* pattern.

## Key learnings

- Re-certification cost is the operative consequence of late-stage regulatory awareness.
- Predates the **EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230** — which [[inspection-robots]] subsequently examines as the post-EU-revision update of this same domain.
- Swiss product-liability framing for autonomous machinery.
