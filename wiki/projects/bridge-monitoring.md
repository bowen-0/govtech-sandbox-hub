# Sensor-based Bridge Monitoring

Sensor data + AI predictions for infrastructure maintenance. Originally planned with a municipal-footbridge partner; after that partner withdrew, the project was carried out at the **Reidholz railway bridge in Wädenswil** in partnership with *Schweizerische Südostbahn AG* (SOB).

## Outcome

Sensors captured **real loads from train traffic for the first time**, providing a precise data-based picture of the bridge's structural condition. The project then developed scenarios for the bridge's remaining service life, supporting concrete maintenance and investment decisions. Demonstrates how data-driven approaches make critical-infrastructure monitoring safer, more efficient, and more sustainable — particularly in the heavily-utilised Metropolitan Area of Zurich.

## Key learnings

- **Data-driven complements standards-based**: AI-supported monitoring does not replace human structural-engineering expertise — the booklet repeats this framing across multiple Phase II projects.
- **Partner withdrawal → pivot** is a legitimate sandbox execution pattern: the project preserved value by rerouting to a different partner rather than aborting.
- Liability and data-protection regime for sensor-based monitoring systems was clarified — direct legal carry-over from [[infrastructure-maintenance]] (Phase I) but with a different physical modality (sensor vs. drone imagery).
