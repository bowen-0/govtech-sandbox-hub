# AI in Education

Legal best-practices framework for AI deployment in classroom and educational settings. The Phase I project that surfaces the data-protection-of-minors angle and the **school-IT-access partner bottleneck** — schools' IT environments are not easily integrated with external AI services.

## Outcome

Published legal best-practices guide.

## Key learnings

- Data-protection of minors is the dominant regulatory consideration in education-sector AI.
- The privacy-by-design lexicon (low resolution, masking, edge computing, instant deletion, data minimisation) — first articulated in [[smart-parking]] and reused here — proves to be cross-sectoral.
- **School-IT access** as a specific instance of the partner-bottleneck pattern.
- One of the Phase II discontinued projects ("AI-supported platform for upper secondary school examinations", per [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03 Additional Projects) attempted to extend this work but was discontinued by mutual agreement — see [[gogymi]] for the partner involved.
