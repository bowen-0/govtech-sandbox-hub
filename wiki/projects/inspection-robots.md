# Autonomous Inspection Robots

`analysis-only` Phase II project — a structured regulatory pre-flight for **EU market access** of Swiss autonomous-robotics products. Anchored on the use case of [[anybotics]] (Swiss autonomous inspection robots in critical infrastructure). No suitable test partner could be identified for operational deployment, so the project focused on **in-depth regulatory analysis** — which the sandbox treats as a successful outcome alongside [[medical-documentation]].

## Outcome

- A **structured regulatory classification** of autonomous inspection systems within the interplay of the **EU AI Act** and the **EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230**.
- Practice-oriented compliance strategies for Swiss vendors targeting the EU market.
- Tested an AI governance platform from [[modulos]] against the **ISO/IEC 42001** AI management-system standard.
- First-time **cross-border sandbox collaboration** with Germany's *Bundesnetzagentur* and Liechtenstein's *Office for Digital Innovation*.

## Key learnings

- The interaction between the AI Act and the EU Machinery Regulation is dynamically evolving — single-instrument analysis is insufficient for multi-regulation AI products.
- ISO/IEC 42001 is concretely operationalisable on a real use case, not only as a paper standard.
- The "EU market access via Swiss sandbox" pattern is a viable model — relevant for the Phase III ecosystem strategy ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §05, "international networking").
