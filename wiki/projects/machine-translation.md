# Machine Translation for Public Administration

Recommendations for public-administration adoption of machine translation for official documents. Examines the Swiss legal stack (cantonal IDG / federal FADP / **commissioned-data-processing contracts**) when administrative text flows through a third-party MT service.

## Outcome

The downstream **commercial-register-extracts MT solution** was successfully rolled out and scaled to multiple cantons including **Kanton Schwyz** — cited in [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §01 as a Phase I roll-out success alongside [[smart-parking]].

## Key learnings

- Established the legal pattern for **commissioned data processing** (*Auftragsdatenbearbeitung*) for third-party AI services touching administrative text data.
- Articulated the FADP Art. 21 / Constitution Art. 29 framing for automated administrative decisions — re-used later in [[building-permits]].
- Demonstrates the cross-cantonal scaling pathway for sandbox outputs (KZH → KSZ).
