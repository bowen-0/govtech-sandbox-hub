# Canton Zurich AI Ecosystem Strategy 2026–2029 — 4-Pillar Strategy

The strategic frame that the AI Innovation Sandbox now sits within. Following from Phase II, the **Government Council of the Canton of Zurich** decided to strengthen the AI ecosystem with a four-pillar approach for 2026–2029 — operationalised through the *Amt für Wirtschaft* and partners. This is the most up-to-date macro-context for any hackathon output: the audience of the sandbox knowledge is no longer just "people running a pilot" but "people executing on one of four ecosystem pillars."

## Why this matters for the hackathon

The strategy's existence reframes the audience question:

1. **Pillar I — Sandbox.** Continues, but with refinements (per booklet §06 "Conclusion and Outlook"): more agile project intake, differentiation of project types (analysis vs. testing), thematic calls aligned to prioritised areas (e.g. financial sector for ZH), structured access to insights (thematic playbooks, one-stop shop, AI chatbot interaction surface), public dialogue.
2. **Pillar II — Start-up Support.** Physical AI hubs and scaling support.
3. **Pillar III — SME Adoption.** Knowledge transfer + upskilling. **The most relevant pillar for "a non-technical administrator planning a new AI pilot"** — the original challenge persona maps to this pillar.
4. **Pillar IV — Public Dialogue.** Already established in 10+ municipalities; being systematised and expanded.

## Booklet's closing framing

> *"The AI sandbox thus marks not a conclusion, but the starting point of a long-term AI ecosystem strategy. It has demonstrated how AI can be responsibly tested, appropriately contextualised within regulatory frameworks, and made practically usable, thereby laying the foundation for the next phase of development of the AI ecosystem in Zurich through 2029."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §06, p. 25.
